# Sideboard & Companion Rules (CR 100.4 & 702.139)

Rules governing the construction and in-game usage of Sideboards and Companions across tournament and casual play.

---

## 1. Sideboard Rules (CR 100.4 & MTR 3.15)

### Constructed Format Limits
- A sideboard may contain **at most 15 cards** (CR 100.4a).
- A player may choose to play with fewer than 15 sideboard cards or even 0.
- **Combined 4-of Rule:** A player cannot have more than 4 copies of any unique English card name across their **main deck and sideboard combined**.

### Post-Sideboarding Deck Size (MTR 3.15)
- In Constructed tournaments, players modify their decks between games of a match:
  - Players are **not** strictly required to do a 1-for-1 swap.
  - Players may side in more cards than they side out, as long as:
    1. The main deck remains at or above the minimum deck size (60 cards).
    2. The sideboard does not exceed 15 cards.
  - *Example:* A player with a 60-card main deck and 15-card sideboard may side in 4 cards and side out 0, playing Game 2 with a 64-card main deck and an 11-card sideboard.

### Cards "From Outside the Game" (CR 400.11 / MTR 3.15)
- Cards that refer to bringing cards from "outside the game" into the game (e.g., Wish cards like *Burning Wish*, *Living Wish*, or cards with the *Learn* keyword fetching *Lessons*):
  - In **sanctioned tournament play**, "outside the game" is strictly restricted to the player's **sideboard**.
  - In **Commander (CR 903.11)**, official Commander rules do not use a sideboard; cards from outside the game do not function unless an explicit local playgroup rule is established.

---

## 2. Companion Rules (CR 702.139)

The Companion mechanic allows a player to reveal one creature card from outside the game as a designated Companion before Game 1 begins.

### Deck Construction Requirement
- To designate a Companion, the **entire starting deck** (including the main deck and sideboard) must satisfy the deckbuilding restriction printed on that card.
- Examples:
  - **Lurrus of the Dream-Den:** Each permanent card in your starting deck must have mana value 2 or less.
  - **Yorion, Sky Nomad:** Your starting deck must contain at least 20 cards more than the minimum deck size (e.g., at least 80 cards in Constructed).
  - **Kaheera, the Orphanguard:** Each creature card in your starting deck must be a Cat, Elemental, Nightmare, Dinosaur, or Beast.
  - **Jegantha, the Wellspring:** No card in your starting deck may have more than one of the same mana symbol in its mana cost.
  - **Gyruda, Doom of Depths:** Your starting deck must contain only cards with even mana values.
  - **Obosh, the Preypiercer:** Your starting deck must contain only cards with odd mana values and land cards.

### Rule Errata (Comprehensive Rules Update)
- Under the updated rule (effective June 2020), a companion does **not** cast directly from outside the game.
- **Putting Companion into Hand:** Once during the game, any time you could cast a sorcery (during your main phase when the stack is empty), you may pay `{3}` to put your companion from outside the game into your hand. This is a special action (CR 116.2g) that doesn't use the stack and cannot be responded to.
- Once in hand, it is cast as a normal spell.

### Companion in Sideboard
- In Constructed formats, a designated Companion **occupies 1 of the 15 sideboard slots**.
