# Official Rules: MTG Deck Construction

This reference document synthesizes the official rules from the **Magic: The Gathering Comprehensive Rules** (CR) governing deck construction for both constructed and limited formats.

---

## 1. General Deck Construction Rules (CR 100)

### 100.2a Constructed Play
> "In constructed play (a way of playing in which each player creates their own deck ahead of time), each deck has a minimum deck size of 60 cards. A constructed deck may contain any number of basic land cards and no more than four of any card with a particular English name other than basic land cards."

- **Minimum Deck Size:** 60 cards.
- **Maximum Deck Size:** There is no maximum deck size (CR 100.5), provided the player can shuffle their deck unassisted within a reasonable timeframe (MTR 3.9).
- **The "4-of" Rule:** A deck may not contain more than 4 copies of any card sharing the same English card name across the **combined main deck and sideboard**.
- **Basic Land Exemption:** Any number of basic land cards may be included:
  - `Plains`, `Island`, `Swamp`, `Mountain`, `Forest`
  - `Snow-Covered Plains`, `Snow-Covered Island`, `Snow-Covered Swamp`, `Snow-Covered Mountain`, `Snow-Covered Forest`
  - `Wastes`

### 100.2b Limited Play (Draft / Sealed)
> "In limited play (a way of playing in which each player gets the same quantity of unopened Magic product such as booster packs and creates their own deck using only this product and basic land cards), each deck has a minimum deck size of 40 cards. A limited deck may contain as many duplicates of a card as are included with the product."

- **Minimum Deck Size:** 40 cards.
- **Copy Limit:** None. If a player opens or drafts 6 copies of a card, all 6 may be included.
- **Card Pool:** Players may add any number of basic lands to their limited pool.

### 100.4 Sideboards
> "Each player may also have a sideboard, which is a group of additional cards the player may use to modify their deck between games of a match."

- **100.4a Constructed:** Sideboard may contain **at most 15 cards**. The 4-card limit (CR 100.2a) applies across the **combined deck and sideboard**.
- **100.4b Limited:** In individual limited play, all cards in a player's card pool not in the main deck constitute their sideboard.
- **100.4c / 100.4d Multiplayer:** Assigned per player; cards cannot be transferred between team members.

## 2. Magic Golden Rules & Exceptions (CR 101)

### 101.1 Card Text Overrides Rules
> "Whenever a card's text directly contradicts these rules, the card takes precedence."

Specific cards override the maximum copy limit:
- **Relentless Rats:** *"A deck can have any number of cards named Relentless Rats."*
- **Shadowborn Apostle:** *"A deck can have any number of cards named Shadowborn Apostle."*
- **Persistent Petitioners:** *"A deck can have any number of cards named Persistent Petitioners."*
- **Dragon's Approach:** *"A deck can have any number of cards named Dragon's Approach."*
- **Rat Colony:** *"A deck can have any number of cards named Rat Colony."*
- **Seven Dwarves:** *"A deck can have up to seven cards named Seven Dwarves."*

## 3. Commander / EDH Rules (CR 903)

### 903.3 Commander Designation
- Each deck has a **legendary creature** card designated as its commander.
- **Planeswalker Commanders (903.3a):** A planeswalker card may only be a commander if it explicitly includes text stating *"This card can be your commander"*.
- **Partners (CR 702.124):** A player may designate two commanders if both have the `Partner`, `Partner with [name]`, or `Friends Forever` ability.

### 903.4 Color Identity
- The color identity of a card is determined by:
  1. Mana symbols in its mana cost.
  2. Mana symbols in its rules text (activated abilities, triggered abilities).
  3. Color indicators (CR 204).
  4. Characteristic-defining abilities (CR 604.3).
  5. Both faces of a double-faced or meld card (CR 903.4d).
- **Reminder text is ignored** when determining color identity (CR 903.4c). Example: *Extort* reminder text `{W/B}` does not add Black to a White card.

### 903.5 Commander Deck Construction Restrictions
- **903.5a Exact Deck Size:** Each deck must contain **exactly 100 cards**, including its commander(s).
- **903.5b Singleton Rule:** Other than basic lands, each card in a Commander deck must have a different English name (no duplicates allowed).
- **903.5c Color Identity Inclusion:** Every card in the deck must have a color identity that is a subset of the commander's color identity. A card cannot contain any colored mana symbols not in the commander's identity.
- **903.5d Basic Land Types:** A card with a basic land type may be included only if each color of mana it could produce is included in the commander's color identity.

### 903.8 Commander Tax
- When cast from the command zone, a commander costs an additional `{2}` for each previous time the player has cast it from the command zone that game.

### 903.10 Commander Damage
- A player dealt **21 or more combat damage** by the same commander over the course of the game loses the game as a state-based action (CR 704).

## 4. Brawl Option (CR 903.12)

- **Deck Size:** Exactly 60 cards, including its commander.
- **Commander:** Legendary Creature OR Legendary Planeswalker.
- **Format Pool:** Cards legal in Standard.
- **Singleton:** Max 1 of any non-basic card.
- **Color Identity:** Color identity restrictions apply.
- **Commander Damage:** Brawl does NOT use the 21 commander damage rule (CR 903.12h).
