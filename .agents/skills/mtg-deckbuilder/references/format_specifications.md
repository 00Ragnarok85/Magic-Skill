## Detailed Format Breakdown

### 1. Standard
- **Scope:** Rotating constructed format including premier sets from the most recent ~3 years.
- **Deck Requirements:** Minimum 60 cards mainboard, up to 15 sideboard cards.
- **Card Limit:** Max 4 copies of any unique English card name across main + sideboard (excluding basic lands).
- **Banlist:** Maintained by Wizards of the Coast.

### 2. Pioneer
- **Scope:** Non-rotating constructed format comprising standard-legal sets from *Return to Ravnica* forward.
- **Deck Requirements:** Minimum 60 cards mainboard, up to 15 sideboard cards.
- **Card Limit:** 4-of rule applies across main + sideboard.
- **Special Exclusions:** Does not include Modern Horizons or supplementary straight-to-Modern sets.

### 3. Modern
- **Scope:** Non-rotating constructed format comprising sets from *Eighth Edition* (2003) forward, plus *Modern Horizons* releases and *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth*.
- **Deck Requirements:** Minimum 60 cards mainboard, up to 15 sideboard cards.
- **Card Limit:** 4-of rule applies across main + sideboard.

### 4. Legacy
- **Scope:** Eternal constructed format allowing cards from all black-bordered and white-bordered tournament sets across Magic's history.
- **Deck Requirements:** Minimum 60 cards mainboard, up to 15 sideboard cards.
- **Card Limit:** 4-of rule applies across main + sideboard.
- **Banned List:** Extensive banlist banning power outliers (e.g., Power Nine, fast mana, dexterity cards).

### 5. Vintage
- **Scope:** Eternal format allowing cards from all of Magic's history.
- **Deck Requirements:** Minimum 60 cards mainboard, up to 15 sideboard cards.
- **Restricted List:** Unique to Vintage. Cards are not banned for power reasons; instead, cards on the Restricted List may only be played as **exactly 1 copy** combined across main deck and sideboard (e.g. Black Lotus, Ancestral Recall, Time Walk, Sol Ring).

### 6. Commander / EDH (CR 903)
- **Scope:** 100-card singleton multiplayer casual/competitive format.
- **Deck Requirements:** Exactly 100 cards including commander(s).
- **Commander:** Legendary Creature (or Planeswalker with commander text).
- **Color Identity:** All 99 cards must adhere to the commander's color identity.
- **Sideboard:** 0 cards in sanctioned Commander (wishes do not fetch cards).

### 7. Pauper
- **Scope:** Eternal constructed format where **every card must have been printed as a Common** in a paper or digital release.
- **Deck Requirements:** Minimum 60 cards mainboard, up to 15 sideboard cards.
- **Card Limit:** 4-of rule applies across main + sideboard.

### 8. Limited (Booster Draft & Sealed Deck) (CR 100.2b)
- **Scope:** Players open booster packs and construct decks using only their card pool + basic lands.
- **Deck Requirements:** Minimum 40 cards mainboard.
- **Sideboard:** Every card in the player's pool that is not in the main deck is part of the sideboard. Continuous sideboarding is permitted in casual play.
